The most common use-cases and domains for customer assistance typically include:

1. **E-Commerce:**
   - Order processing and tracking
   - Returns and refunds
   - Product inquiries and comparisons
   - Account management

2. **Technical Support:**
   - Troubleshooting issues with software/hardware
   - Installation guides and setup assistance
   - Performance optimization tips
   - Bug reporting and resolution tracking

3. **Finance and Banking:**
   - Online banking assistance
   - Transaction inquiries
   - Loan application processes
   - Fraud detection and reporting

4. **Travel and Hospitality:**
   - Booking assistance and cancellations
   - Travel itinerary changes
   - Hotel reservations and inquiries
   - Customer loyalty programs

5. **Telecommunications:**
   - Service activation and deactivation
   - Billing inquiries and disputes
   - Plan changes and upgrades
   - Technical support for devices and connections

6. **Healthcare:**
   - Appointment scheduling
   - Prescription inquiries and refills
   - Insurance claim support
   - Patient health records access

7. **Education:**
   - Course registration and information
   - Technical support for online learning platforms
   - Financial aid queries
   - Alumni services and support

Each of these domains has its unique challenges and customer needs, leading to distinct FAQs, troubleshooting steps, and customer service workflows.
